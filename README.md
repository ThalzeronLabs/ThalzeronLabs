### 👋 About me

I build Android apps that ship — and I put AI inside them: subscriptions and AI assistants in apps live on Google
Play, and **large language models running fully on the phone**, offline. Most of my product code lives in
private repositories; the projects below are case studies, and the pinned repositories are open showcases.

🟢 **Available for freelance work** — native Android apps with AI inside: LLM features, on-device models, Play Billing and Google Play publishing. Async-first, everything in writing.
